FFmpeg
======

This application bundles FFmpeg libraries (avcodec, avdevice, avfilter,
avformat, avutil, swresample, swscale), version 63.x, in
`assets/ffmpeg/` and inside `assets/ffmpeg.zip`.

FFmpeg is free software licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License (LGPL) version 2.1 or later:
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html

FFmpeg source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

TouchMirror accesses FFmpeg through the FFmpeg.AutoGen bindings
(MIT license): https://github.com/Ruslan-B/FFmpeg.AutoGen

The FFmpeg binaries are used unmodified. You may replace the DLLs in
`assets/ffmpeg/` with your own LGPL-compatible FFmpeg build.
